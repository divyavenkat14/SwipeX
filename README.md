# 🚀 SwipeX

## AI-Integrated Swipe-Based Intelligent Job Discovery and Career Assistance Platform

SwipeX is a full-stack, **AI-integrated career assistance and job discovery platform** designed to make finding and applying for jobs more interactive, personalized, and intelligent.

Unlike traditional job portals that primarily rely on keyword-based searching and filtering, SwipeX combines a **swipe-based job discovery experience with ATS resume analysis, skill matching, job recommendations, and career assistance**.

The platform provides dedicated experiences for **Job Seekers, Recruiters, and Administrators**, with secure authentication, role-based access, job management, company management, resume management, applications, and intelligent job-matching features.

## 📌 About SwipeX

Traditional job portals often require users to repeatedly search, filter, and browse through large numbers of job listings. Even after finding a job, users may not know how well their resume matches the requirements of that particular position.

SwipeX addresses this problem by combining **interactive job discovery with intelligent resume analysis**.

The platform analyzes the skills present in a user's resume against the skills required for a job and generates an **ATS compatibility score**. These scores are then used to help prioritize and recommend relevant job opportunities.

# ✨ Key Features

## 👤 Job Seeker

Job seekers can:

- Create an account.
- Log in securely using JWT authentication.
- Create and manage their profile.
- Manage resume information.
- Manage skills.
- Discover available job opportunities.
- Search and filter jobs.
- Explore jobs using a swipe-based interface.
- Save or skip job opportunities.
- Apply for suitable positions.
- Access their personalized dashboard.

---

## 🏢 Recruiter

Recruiters can:

- Create a recruiter account.
- Log in securely.
- Access a recruiter-specific dashboard.
- Create and manage company information.
- Post job opportunities.
- Manage existing job postings.
- View candidate applications.
- Access recruiter-protected functionality.

---

## 🛡️ Administrator

Administrators have access to platform-level information and management features.

The administrator dashboard provides:

- Total users
- Total recruiters
- Total job seekers
- Total companies
- Total jobs
- Total applications

Administrators can also view:

- Users
- Companies
- Jobs
- Reports
